Privacy Statement

This app works completely offline. Your subtitle files never leave your device: conversion happens locally in the app, and no file contents, file names, or usage data are collected, stored, transmitted, or shared with anyone. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers, and it has no in-app purchases. Questions can be sent through the contact information on the app's Microsoft Store listing page.
